Everything in its place.

Practical business organisation without the complexity of a full accounting suite.

♙

Customer directory

Store names, companies, email addresses, phone numbers and notes in one searchable list.

▤

Invoice tracking

Create and edit invoices, track draft, sent and paid status, and see outstanding totals.

↗

Expense records

Record spending by date and category, include supplier details and export your records.

✓

Task manager & recurring work

Capture upcoming work, set priorities, set daily, weekly or monthly repeats and mark tasks complete.

▧

Document links

Keep contracts, receipts, proposals and reference files listed in one place, ready to open.

▥

Business analytics

Review paid income, outstanding invoices, six-month cashflow, invoice ageing and spending by category.

Choose five colour themes, five fonts, bold or regular text, and dark, light or system appearance.

Local-first data

Built-in guidance

Windows conveniences

Your business is personal.
Your tools should be clear.

SoloDesk is a Windows-first organiser for freelancers and small businesses. It focuses on practical records and a calm interface, with local SQLite storage and no account or cloud sync requirement.
